> Re: naming of the app — we propose "CompassBot" 🧭

## Body

Hi [Name],

First of all, thank you for flagging the name issue. It is a fair and important catch,
and it made me revisit it properly rather than shrug it off.

Quick recap of what you pointed out: **"CounselorBot"** reads differently in English —
"counselor" immediately suggests therapy or a certified counselling role, so the app
sounds like (or over-promises) a clinical service. Paired with "SBS" it also collided
with an unrelated well-known brand.

I have a proposal and would like your honest opinion before I commit to it.

### The proposal

1. **Name the product "CompassBot".**
   - The compass is already the heart of the app: our orientation guide is literally
     called "Bussola" (compass). The product would finally be named after its own core idea.
   - It keeps the "-bot" ending on purpose: we want it to be clear this is an AI assistant.
   - It drops "SBS" from the public name.
   - Tagline, e.g.: *"CompassBot — find your direction in study and life."*

2. **Call the AI personas "helmsmen" (timonieri) instead of "counselors".**
   - Same compass metaphor: a helmsman steers the course *with* you, without pretending
     to be a therapist or a certified counsellor.
   - The personas keep their own names (Clio, Giulio, Iride, …) — "helmsman" is just
     what they *are*.
   - In Italian: *Timoniere*.

### What I'd like from you

- **Does this resolve the concern you raised?** Specifically: does "CompassBot" still
  suggest anything you dislike to an English ear? And does "helmsman" fix the
  "counselor = therapy" reading?
- If anything still bothers you, what is it? I would rather hear it now than after the rename.
- If you prefer a different prefix, backups I'm considering are **HelmsBot**
  (the helmsman made explicit), **NorthBot** and **LumenBot** — let me know if any of
  them works better for you.

Thank you again for taking the time on this — it made the app better before it even changed.
