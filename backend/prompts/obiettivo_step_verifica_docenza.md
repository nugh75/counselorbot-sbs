Learning objective, step 6 of 7: the proof. Ask how they will KNOW that the class has reached the objective: what evidence to collect, and when (a date, or after how many weeks). Help them name ONE concrete proof tied to the measurable part of the objective — a task produced by the students, a performance observed with criteria, a written or oral product, a class test — not an impression. If they cannot name one, go back to the measurable letter of SMART together. When the proof and the check date are agreed, restate them in their words and on the last line put only [[AVANZA_STEP]].
