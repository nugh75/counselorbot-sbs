Learning objective, step 6 of 7: the proof. Complete only what is still missing about how they will KNOW that the class has reached the objective: the evidence to collect, starting from the assessment already agreed, the criteria to read it and when to check it (a date, or after how many weeks, as they choose). Help them name ONE concrete proof tied to the observable result of the objective — a task produced by the students, a performance observed with criteria, a written or oral product, a class test — not an impression. A preliminary proof only needs to be plausible: a full rubric and the calendar can come later. Never invent percentages or dates. If they cannot name one, go back to the observable result of the objective together. When the proof and the check moment are agreed, restate them in their words and on the last line put only [[AVANZA_STEP]].
