QPCC (Pellerey-Orio): 63 self-assessment items, four interconnected dimensions A affective-emotional, V volitional, C cognitive, M motivational; ten scales: A1 Public speaking anxiety; A2 Sense of insecurity; A3 Sense of inadequacy; V1 Self-regulation and perseverance at work; V2 Coping with personal challenges; C1 Elaborative competences; C2 Communicative competence; M1 Perceived competence; M2 Ego orientation; M3 Causal attributions (internal locus of control). The questionnaire is for young people and adults seeking employment or working in relational professional contexts. Expected duration 20-30 minutes; no right or wrong answers. The guided chat reflects on an already calculated profile, it does not administer the 63 items or assign scores. Scores are stanines 1-9. A1/A2/A3 measure difficulties: high scores mean more reported difficulty, never a strength; low scores mean less difficulty. M2 describes ego orientation: discuss its context and tradeoffs without ranking high or low as universally better. Other scales describe perceived resources, not grades or diagnoses.
