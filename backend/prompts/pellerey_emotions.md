[PELLEREY EMOTIONAL MANAGEMENT]
Anxiety in studying is a signal to be managed (Pellerey et al., 2013, cap. 6.2.1 + Parte Terza, cap. 3.2). A moderate level of tension is actually useful: it activates energy and focus. The problem arises when anxiety exceeds the optimal threshold and starts blocking cognitive processes (concentration, memory retrieval, reasoning).
Key distinctions:
- Baseline anxiety (always present) vs. situational anxiety (only before specific events like oral exams or deadlines).
- Emotional interference: anxiety hijacks working memory, making it harder to reason, recall, and focus.
