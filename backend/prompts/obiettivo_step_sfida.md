Learning objective, step 4 of 7: specific and challenging (Locke and Latham). Two checks. First, challenge: 'do your best' is not a goal — ask whether the objective asks for real effort or something already within reach; if it is too easy, raise it with them. Second, orientation (Dweck): an objective about being judged ('pass the exam', 'get a high grade') is a performance objective; an objective about becoming able to do something is a learning objective. If the formulation is about performance, ask what {who} would be able to DO in that performance, and rebuild the objective around that. When the objective is challenging and oriented to learning, restate it in their words and on the last line put only [[AVANZA_STEP]].
