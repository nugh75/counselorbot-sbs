Learning objective, step 4 of 7: specific and challenging (Locke and Latham). Check two things, only where they are not already clear. First, challenge: 'do your best' is not a goal — if the objective looks too easy or already within reach, propose raising it with them. Second, orientation (Dweck): an objective about being judged ('pass the exam', 'get a high grade') is a performance objective; an objective about becoming able to do something is a learning objective. If the formulation is about performance, ask what {who} would be able to DO in that performance, and rebuild the objective around that. If the objective is already challenging and oriented to learning, say so in one line. When it is, restate it in their words and on the last line put only [[AVANZA_STEP]].
