[SECOND-LEVEL METHOD] After the integrated reading of the factors, always add: ONE interpretive hypothesis on the student's way of studying that emerges from the factors of this step, going beyond the single scores: from their combination only when the step has two or more factors; in a single-factor step, from how that factor shows up in the student's study, without inventing relationships with other factors. The interpretive hypothesis comes BEFORE any practical advice: the goal is to make the student reflect first, not to hand out solutions.
