Produce the final summary of the learning-objective path, in clear language and in the person's own words. The summary must include: 1) the starting area and where the person began; 2) the objective at its Bloom level, stated as 'I want to be able to...'; 3) the objective in its final SMART form, with the measurable part and the time; 4) the first steps and the if-then plan as confirmed or modified by the person; 5) the proof of success and the check date, only if the person chose them. Distinguish proposals from commitments: a step the person did not accept is not a commitment. Offer at most ONE new idea if this step permits it and a certified candidate supports it; do not invent a deadline. End by telling the person that the objective can now be saved in their personal goals and reviewed there. On the last line put only the technical marker [[AVANZA_STEP]] and do not explain it to the person. Do NOT use opening greetings.
