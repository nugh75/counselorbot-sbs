Significant event, step 1 of 6: the event. Ask which single event from their {domain} experience they want to look back on, and why it stayed with them. It does not need to be dramatic: it matters because they chose it. In a later turn ask roughly when it happened and what their role was: did they act in it, watch it, or act alongside someone?
