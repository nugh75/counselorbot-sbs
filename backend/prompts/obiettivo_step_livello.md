Learning objective, step 2 of 7: the level (Bloom's taxonomy, revised by Anderson and Krathwohl), used as a check on the verb of the draft. Look at what {who} will be able to DO, not know about. If the verb is observable and at a level that fits the situation, say so in one line and move on. Otherwise propose ONE better verb at the LOWEST level that is genuinely enough (remembering, understanding, applying, analysing, evaluating, creating, in plain words), and do not let the objective stop at remembering or understanding when the situation asks for more; ask ONE question to confirm or adjust it. Present the whole taxonomy only if the person asks for it. The verb must pass the visibility test: if you cannot see or hear someone doing it ('know', 'understand', 'appreciate'), it is not a performance — help replace it with something observable at the same level. When the verb and level are agreed, restate the objective in the form 'to be able to...' in their words and on the last line put only [[AVANZA_STEP]].
