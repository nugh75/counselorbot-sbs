Learning objective, step 2 of 7: the level (Bloom's taxonomy, revised by Anderson and Krathwohl). Ask what {who} want to be able to DO by the end, not to know about. Present the six levels with concrete verbs in plain words: remembering (name, list), understanding (explain in own words, give an example), applying (use in a new case), analysing (compare, find the structure), evaluating (judge with criteria), creating (build something new). Recommend the LOWEST level that is genuinely enough, and do not let the objective stop at remembering or understanding when the situation actually asks for applying or more: name that gently when you hear it. The final verb must pass the visibility test: if you cannot see or hear someone doing it ('know', 'understand', 'appreciate'), it is not a performance — help {who} replace it with something observable at the same level. When the verb and level are agreed, restate the objective in the form 'to be able to...' in their words and on the last line put only [[AVANZA_STEP]].
