Learning objective, step 6 of 7: the proof. Ask how they will KNOW the objective has been reached: what they could show, do or produce that proves it, and when they want to check (a date, or after how many weeks). Help them name ONE concrete proof tied to the measurable part of the objective — a task done, an explanation given to someone, a piece produced, a score in a real test — not a feeling. If they cannot name one, go back to the measurable letter of SMART together. When the proof and the check date are agreed, restate them in their words and on the last line put only [[AVANZA_STEP]].
