INSTRUMENTS SHEET (canonical data; use EXACT names, acronyms and numbers from here):
- QSA — Learning Strategies Questionnaire (Pellerey, 100 items). 14 factors, stanine scale 1-9.
  Cognitive: C1 Elaborative strategies · C2 Self-regulation · C3 Disorientation · C4 Willingness to collaborate · C5 Use of semantic organisers · C6 Concentration difficulties · C7 Self-questioning.
  Affective: A1 Basic anxiety · A2 Volition · A3 Attribution to controllable causes · A4 Attribution to uncontrollable causes · A5 Lack of perseverance · A6 Perception of competence · A7 Emotional interference.
  Inverted factors (high score = area for growth, not strength): C3, C6, A1, A4, A5, A7.
- QSAr — Reduced QSA. 8 factors: C1r Elaborative strategies · C2r Self-regulatory strategies · C3r Graphic strategies and semantic organizers · C4r Lack of attention control (inv) · A1r Anxiety and emotional control (inv) · A2r Volition · A3r Causal attributions · A4r Perception of competence.
- ZTPI — Zimbardo Time Perspective Inventory (by Philip Zimbardo, integrated in the project). 5 perspectives: T1 Past Negative (inv) · T2 Past Positive · T3 Present Hedonistic · T4 Present Fatalistic (inv) · T5 Future. Profile ideal = 'balanced time perspective' (Zimbardo), readapted on Italian sample (Margottini).
- QPCS — Questionnaire on the Perception of one's own Strategic Competences. 5 factors: S1 Managing emotions · S2 Communicative competence · S3 Will and perseverance · S4 Strategies and collaboration · S5 Confidence and life project.
- QPCC — Questionnaire on the Perception of one's own Competences and Beliefs. 5 factors: K1 Public communication · K2 Managing anxiety and responsibility · K3 Volition and self-regulation · K4 Elaboration strategies · K5 Beliefs about oneself.
- QAP — Career Adaptability Questionnaire. 4 factors: AD1 Future orientation · AD2 Control and autonomy · AD3 Curiosity and exploration · AD4 Confidence and problem solving.
- Savickas — narrative career construction interview (M. Savickas); resource of THIS platform, not of competenzestrategiche.it.
- IDEA — conversation tool of CounselorBot for a concrete idea, decision or project; produces a cumulative map, without questionnaire items or scores.
- EVENTO_STUDIO — Evento significativo di studio / Significant study event: a CounselorBot conversation about one study episode (lesson, exam, group project), without questionnaire items or scores.
- EVENTO_PROFESSIONALE — Evento significativo professionale / Significant professional event: a CounselorBot conversation about one work or placement episode, including a teacher's practice, without questionnaire items or scores.
- OBIETTIVO_STUDIO — Il mio obiettivo di apprendimento / My learning objective: a guided conversation that sets one learning objective (Bloom level, SMART, plan, proof), without questionnaire items or scores.
- OBIETTIVO_DOCENZA — Obiettivi per la mia classe / Objectives for my class: the teacher's guided conversation for one didactic objective aligned with activities and assessment.
  Both significant-event tools follow six steps (event, facts, what worked, what did not, a second look, next time) plus the separate final summary at step 7. Step 6 is what to try next time, not the summary. The summary is a draft milestone for the Timeline that the person reviews, edits and explicitly saves; there is no automatic saving or diagnosis. These are conversation tools, not calendar entries or questionnaires from competenzestrategiche.it.
