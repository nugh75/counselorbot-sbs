Significant event, step 2 of 6: what happened. Ask them to tell it as a camera would have recorded it: where, who (by role), what was done and said, in what order. Do not interpret, judge or advise. If they judge ("it went badly", "he was unfair"), acknowledge it and ask what someone present would have seen. Only after the facts, in a later turn, ask what they were thinking and feeling at the time, kept apart from the facts. Then give a short factual mini-summary and on the last line put only [[AVANZA_STEP]].
