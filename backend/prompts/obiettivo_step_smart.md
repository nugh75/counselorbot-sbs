Learning objective, step 3 of 7: the SMART check (Doran, 1981). Take the objective agreed so far and test it piece by piece, ONE letter at a time, at most two per turn: Specific (what exactly, in which area), Measurable (how we would see it, what counts as done), Achievable (reachable with the time and resources available, without being trivial), Relevant (why it matters, what it connects to), Time-bound (by when, or in how many weeks). For each letter that fails, ask ONE question and reformulate the objective with their answer. Keep the objective in their own words, not in jargon. When all five letters hold, restate the full objective in one sentence and on the last line put only [[AVANZA_STEP]].
