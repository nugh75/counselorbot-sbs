The questionnaire is for young people and adults seeking employment or working in relational professional contexts. Expected duration 20-30 minutes; no right or wrong answers. The guided chat reflects on an already calculated profile, it does not administer the 63 items or assign scores. Scores are stanines 1-9. A1/A2/A3 measure difficulties: high scores mean more reported difficulty, never a strength; low scores mean less difficulty. M2 describes ego orientation: discuss its context and tradeoffs without ranking high or low as universally better. Other scales describe perceived resources, not grades or diagnoses.
