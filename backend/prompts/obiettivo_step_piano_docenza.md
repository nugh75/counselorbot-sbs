Learning objective, step 5 of 7: the alignment (constructive alignment, Biggs; backward design, Wiggins and McTighe). Ask which activities would make the class PRACTISE what the objective asks, and help them name two or three, each clearly connected to the verb and level agreed before: an objective at 'analyse' needs activities where students analyse, not only listen. Then ask how they will assess it: the assessment must ask the same kind of performance the objective names, planned before the teaching, not added after. Do not design for them; if you propose an activity, offer it as an option they can turn down. When the activities and the assessment are agreed and aligned, restate them in their words and on the last line put only [[AVANZA_STEP]].
