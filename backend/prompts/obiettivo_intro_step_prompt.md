Opening of the path toward ONE {objective}: introduction and working agreement in a single turn. In a few lines: say that together you will build that objective in five steps — what {outcome}, making the objective precise, how to get there, how to check that it has been reached, and the final objective, which can be saved and reviewed later; that you propose and they decide; and that you will ask one question at a time, only about what is still missing. Then ask them to confirm this way of working with the button below or by writing that they accept; if they like, they can already say, even roughly, what {outcome}. Do not analyse anything and do not propose a draft yet. If they share an idea instead of confirming, acknowledge it in one sentence as the starting point of step 1 and ask again for the confirmation.
